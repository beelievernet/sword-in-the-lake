Sword in the Lake

The overworld has grown: a town with a shop and villagers, the Lost Woods, open Hyrule Field, the sandy Gerudo Drylands, and Lake Hylia's shore — all connected by dirt roads. A banner names each zone as you enter it. A cave on the lake's north shore leads down into the Lakebed Cavern. Somewhere in the lake, a sword waits — but that claim is a long way off.

📱 On phone
Touch anywhere on the left side — a floating stick appears under your thumb. Gold button swings the sword; the round ITEM button uses whatever you've equipped from your bag.
🎯 Free-angle attacks
You attack at the exact angle you're moving — diagonals and everything in between, not just up/down/left/right. Stop moving and you keep facing that angle, so you can line up a swing, boomerang, or arrow and fire standing still.
🧭 Direction indicator
A small gold arrow at your feet always points where you're aiming. Your sprite only faces 4 ways, so trust the arrow — it's your true aim for sword, items, and shield.
🛡️ Your shield
Always equipped, no button to hold. It only blocks ranged rocks coming at you from the front — inside the cone you're aiming toward. Rocks from the side or behind still hit, and it never blocks bumping into an enemy. During any attack it drops entirely: your shield swings out to your side while you swing the sword, throw the boomerang, or shoot the bow, so a frontal rock gets through until that attack ends.
⌨️ On desktop
WASD / arrows (hold two keys for a diagonal aim), Space sword, K use equipped item, L bag, E shop.
🏪 The Shop
In town (gold roof). Walk up to the door and tap Enter Shop: boomerang 💎60, bow 💎120, arrows, even an extra heart.
🌾 Tall grass
Waist-high patches rustle in the field and woods — you wade through them, half-sunk and slower, like the old games. Slash them for loot; they regrow.
👾 Enemies
Slimes each move in their own pattern: field slimes hop and pause, woods slimes stalk in bursts, desert slimes telegraph and charge, lake slimes dart. New in the Field: rock-spitters walk a straight line, pause, and spit a rock at you — face the shot with your shield up-front to deflect it.
🕳️ Lakebed Cavern
A cave mouth on Lake Hylia's north shore leads down into a dungeon of single-screen rooms. Locked doors want small keys 🔑 — clear a sealed room to earn one. Bombs 💣 wait in a chest inside: they crack cracked walls (look for the fracture lines) and blast anything close — including you, so step back. Something big guards a Heart Container at the far end.
🗡️ The sword in the lake
A sword rests blade-up on a stone pedestal in the middle of Lake Hylia, glinting in the sun. The water around it is far too deep to wade — you can see it, but something is missing before it can be claimed.
🗺️ Getting around
Follow the dirt roads between zones, and watch the minimap — the gold dot is the shop. Every tree blocks exactly where its trunk is drawn, every rock and cactus at its drawn core — walk right up under a canopy until your feet meet the trunk. Trees or rocks planted touching each other join trunk-to-trunk into one straight bar that follows the trunks, so press diagonally and you'll ease around its rounded ends and off the end of a tree wall smoothly, no catching, no lurch. Trees that only touch at a corner don't join at all — the gap between their trunks is open ground, so you can slip through diagonally. Go around the outside of a clump, never between side-by-side trees.
