Hyrule Field

The overworld has grown: a town with a shop and villagers, the Lost Woods, open Hyrule Field, the sandy Gerudo Drylands, and Lake Hylia's shore — all connected by dirt roads. A banner names each zone as you enter it.

📱 On phone
Touch anywhere on the left side — a floating stick appears under your thumb. Gold button swings the sword; the round ITEM button uses whatever you've equipped from your bag.
⌨️ On desktop
WASD / arrows, Space sword, K use equipped item, L bag, E shop.
🏪 The Shop
In town (gold roof). Walk up to the door and tap Enter Shop: boomerang 💎60, bow 💎120, arrows, even an extra heart.
🌾 Tall grass
Waist-high patches rustle in the field and woods — you wade through them, half-sunk and slower, like the old games. Slash them for loot; they regrow.
👾 Enemies
Each slime moves in its own pattern: field slimes hop and pause, woods slimes stalk in bursts, desert slimes telegraph and charge, lake slimes dart. Sword hits flash, pause the game a beat, and knock them flying.
🗺️ Getting around
Follow the dirt roads between zones, and watch the minimap — the gold dot is the shop.

Tip: In Safari / Chrome use Share → Add to Home Screen to play full-screen like an app.
